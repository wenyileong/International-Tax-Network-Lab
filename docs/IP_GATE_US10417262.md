## 2. Claim structure (from the granted text; paraphrased)

Independent claim 1 (method) recites, in substance:

| Element | Content |
|---|---|
| 1a | receiving first data associated with data points; |
| 1b | receiving a selection of a lens function, a selection of a metric function and a resolution function; |
| 1c | mapping second data to a reference space by applying the selected lens using **matrix-vector multiplication**; |
| 1d | generating a cover of the reference space based (at least in part) on the resolution function; |
| 1e | clustering the data within each cover element using the selected metric, to form nodes; |
| 1f | generating a visualisation comprising the nodes and edges joining nodes that share members. |

Claims 12 (medium) and 23 (system) recite the same steps. Dependent claims add particular metrics in matrix form (cosine, correlation, Hamming, L2: claims 2 to 5 and 13 to 16) and particular lenses expressed through matrix operations (L1 centrality as D·1; L2 centrality; Gaussian density; PCA; metric PCA; MDS: claims 6 to 11 and 17 to 22).

The exact wording of claim 1 should be taken from the USPTO PDF before any conclusion is drawn. Claim construction (for example of "matrix-vector multiplication" and "second data") determines scope.

## 3. Comparison with the planned baseline Mapper (research claim chart)

The plan's baseline is the Mapper method published by Singh, Mémoli and Carlsson (2007): choose a filter (lens), cover its range with overlapping intervals at a chosen resolution, cluster the points in each pre-image, and join clusters that share points.

| Element | Baseline Mapper as planned | Observation |
|---|---|---|
| 1a | Receives a feature matrix. | Present. |
| 1b | Lens, metric and resolution (interval count and overlap) are user choices. | Present in substance. |
| 1c | Common lenses (PCA projection, L1 or L2 centrality from a distance matrix) are computed with matrix-vector products in standard numerical code. | Likely present for those lenses; depends on construction and on how the lens is computed. A lens computed without matrix-vector multiplication (for example a single raw feature column) may fall outside the literal wording. |
| 1d | Overlapping interval cover from resolution settings. | Present. |
| 1e | Clustering within each cover element with the chosen metric. | Present. |
| 1f | Graph of clusters joined where they share members, displayed. | Present. |

**Observation.** Version 1.0 of the plan assumed that a generic Mapper baseline lies outside the patent. On the claim structure above, that assumption is not safe: a baseline Mapper with a PCA or centrality lens computed by matrix-vector multiplication appears to correspond element by element to claim 1, subject to claim construction. Whether the claims are valid over the 2007 Mapper publication and other prior art is a separate question; a granted US patent is presumed valid (35 USC s 282), and validity cannot be assumed away in planning.

## 4. Territoriality and use

* No foreign family members are shown, so the patent's direct reach appears to be the United States: making, using, selling, offering or importing the claimed invention there (35 USC s 271(a)).
* Distributing an executable that performs the claimed method to users in the United States, and use by those users, is the main exposure. Development and use outside the United States are outside the patent's territorial reach, subject to confirmation that there is no foreign counterpart.
* The US common-law experimental-use defence is very narrow and does not cover research with a business or institutional purpose (*Madey v Duke University* 307 F 3d 1351 (Fed Cir 2002)).

## 5. Options for the author's decision

| Option | Description | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| (a) | Include a baseline Mapper in the executable. | Highest exposure while the patent is in force in the United States. |
| (b) | Implement and use TDA only in a separate research module that is not distributed and not included in the executable; distribute results, not the tool. | Keeps the executable clear; research continues. |
| (c) | Rely on an existing open-source Mapper library as an optional dependency, installed by the user and excluded from the executable. | Reduces maintenance; does not by itself remove exposure for US users. |
| (d) | Defer TDA until the patent's status is confirmed (in particular the 7.5-year maintenance fee, s 1), or until a lens design outside the claims is established. | Release 1.0 does not need the TDA engine (plan s 1.4 requires two engines). |
| (e) | Restrict the TDA engine to lenses not computed by matrix-vector multiplication (for example a single feature column) and document the reasoning. | Depends on claim construction; would need a reasoned analysis. |
| (f) | Keep the patent-method engine behind this gate and serve the research questions with methods that do not recite the claimed elements (s 5A). | Does not resolve the gate for Mapper-type methods; avoids it for the alternatives, subject to the landscape search in s 5A. |

## 5A. Option (f): alternative methods (added 22 October 2026)

The project plan (v2.4, s 9.4) compares the alternatives in full. In outline, by reference to the elements in s 2:

| Method | 1a | 1b lens, metric, resolution | 1c lens by matrix-vector multiplication | 1d cover | 1e clustering within cover elements | 1f graph of clusters | Assessment |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Persistent homology (Vietoris–Rips) | present | metric only; no lens or resolution | absent | absent | absent (single-linkage components arise across the whole space, not within cover elements) | absent (output is a diagram or barcode) | Outside claim 1 on its face; strongest option. |
| Network-native persistent homology | present | no lens | absent | absent | absent | absent | As above. |
| Ball Mapper | present | metric and a radius; no lens | absent | present in substance (balls) | present in substance (each ball one node) | present | Literal reading avoided only through the lens elements; equivalence arguable; landmark patents to be read first. |
| Exact Reeb graph or contour tree | present | a scalar function | depends on how computed | absent | absent | a graph of level-set components | Different structure; not planned. |
| UMAP with HDBSCAN | present | metric | the embedding is not a lens over a cover | absent | absent | absent | Outside the claim structure; weaker guarantees; not planned. |
| Mapper with a single-column lens (option (e)) | present | present | arguably absent | present | present | present | Closest to equivalence; exposed to the doctrine of equivalents and to a *Festo* analysis if "matrix-vector multiplication" was added by amendment; not planned. |

**Steps before any public distribution of an alternative.**
1. Landscape search: the Ayasdi and SymphonyAI US portfolio (including the landmark patents US 10,216,828, US 11,397,753 and US 12,032,602) and US patents on persistence computation and its applications; record the search strings, dates and results here.
2. Read the prosecution history of US 10,417,262 to see whether the "matrix-vector multiplication" limitation was added by amendment (relevant to option (e) and to any equivalence argument for Ball Mapper).
3. The author confirms or revises the assessment above.

**Status (release 0.5).** Persistent homology is built as `ph.vietoris_rips` with design constraints D1 to D5 (`docs/specs/ph_vietoris_rips.md`). The patent-method engine `us10417262.tda` remains at `interface` and outside the executable. Steps 1 to 3 are open.

## 6. Recommendation for the build (pending the author's decision)

Keep the patent-method TDA engine at `interface` status and keep any Mapper-type code out of the executable until the author records a decision here. Option (f) may proceed in parallel: from release 0.5 the persistent homology engine is in the executable, for private distribution only until the steps in s 5A are complete. Option (d) costs nothing now, because the TDA engine is the third engine and is not required for release 1.0; it also allows the maintenance-fee position to be checked first.
